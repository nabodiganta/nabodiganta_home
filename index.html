Who we are

A new horizon for people living in poverty.

নবদিগন্ত — নতুন দিগন্তের পথে

Nabodiganta is a new Bangladeshi development organisation, founded in 2026. We bring together education, healthcare, financial inclusion and climate resilience to help families build lasting change — starting in five districts along the Jamuna river belt.

We believe people living in poverty, especially women, are not a problem to be solved but the key to solving poverty.

Health & Nutrition

Microfinance

Climate & Disaster

Cyclone shelters, resilient homes and emergency relief.

Agriculture
