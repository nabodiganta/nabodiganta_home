Relief · 28 Aug 2026 · By Farzana Rahman

72 hours in Kurigram: our first flood response reached 2,500 families

When the rivers of Kurigram rose faster than anyone expected this August, whole villages were cut off within hours. Roads disappeared, tube wells went under water, and families moved onto rooftops and school buildings.

Our teams had pre-positioned relief materials in three upazilas as part of our early-warning plan. That preparation meant the first boats left within 12 hours of the alert.

Day one: reaching the cut-off villages

Local volunteers — many of them members of our village organisations — guided rented country boats through flooded paddy fields to reach families who had not eaten since the previous night.

“We knew exactly which houses had elderly people and newborn babies. That is the power of working with the community.”

What we delivered

  • Dry food packs (rice, lentils, oil, salt) for 2,500 families
  • Water purification tablets and jerry cans
  • Emergency cash transfers via mobile banking
  • Mobile health camps with oral saline and first aid

What comes next

As waters recede, our focus shifts to recovery: repairing latrines and tube wells, replacing lost livestock, and making sure children return to school as quickly as possible.
