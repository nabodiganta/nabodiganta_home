Our story

Why we started Nabodiganta

Nabodiganta was founded in 2026 by development workers, teachers and young volunteers who grew up in rural Bangladesh. We have seen how a classroom, a health visit, a small loan or a safe home can change a family’s future — and how many families along the Jamuna river are still left out.

We chose the name Nabodiganta — "a new horizon" — because we want every family we work with to see new possibilities. We are starting small, in five districts around our home in Tangail, and will grow step by step with the communities we serve.

We are inspired by Bangladesh's NGO tradition — organisations like BRAC, Grameen Bank and ASA that helped the country make remarkable progress in child survival, girls' education and women's economic participation — and we want to carry that work forward for a new generation.

What guides us

Mission, vision & values

Mission

To empower people and communities in situations of poverty, illiteracy, disease and social injustice through holistic, sustainable programmes.

Vision

A Bangladesh free from all forms of exploitation and discrimination, where everyone has the opportunity to realise their potential.

Values

  • Integrity & transparency
  • Inclusion of the most vulnerable
  • Innovation driven by evidence
  • Effectiveness at scale

Where we work

Where we are starting

We are starting in five districts along the Jamuna river belt, where river erosion, floods and poverty hit families hardest. By 2030 we plan to reach ten districts, including the haor wetlands and the coast.
